## Separate-cluster proof
Second disposable PostgreSQL cluster, same pinned image, distinct random administrator credential, no shared Docker network or volume; separate authority head/history retained when primary and local journal restored. CI host/operator can control both, so this is simulated separation, not independent production administration or immutable WORM. No production authority product selected or configured. R2 fixture uses synthetic schema and trusted coordinator with admin sessions; no actor broker or real CRM mutation wired to this path. Ordinary runtime lacks schema/function access. No production key material.

Authority holds epoch sequence, epoch UUID, active/quarantine phase, audit sequence and digest. Operator-only locked head transitions; compare local journal, primary epoch and full audit history against independent head; no auto-init or auto-adoption of unanchored records. Synthetic mutation and full audit/outbox event commit together in PostgreSQL; sequence, predecessor, actor, scope, action, target, correlation, outcome and timestamp included, hashed over PostgreSQL JSONB text bytes with explicit domain (not a general interoperable canonicalization). Verify effect IDs and digest chain before accepting; authority anchor advances under separate authority transaction. Authority unavailability or mismatch denies coordinator. No atomic distributed commit: primary can commit before authority and acknowledgement, in which case stop and preserve evidence; no blind retry or automatic anchoring. Authority row lock serializes cooperative mutation/quarantine operations; read guard not a cross-service atomic release lock. Quarantine/reconcile/activate explicit, new epoch unique, audit must match before activation; interrupted primary epoch update before local advance can be retried, coordinated rollback with older epoch/head denied. Does not quarantine legacy proof paths or all application access. No production cutover or mutation acceptance.

## Proposed production authority contract (D11-05 pending)
Customer-controlled authority in distinct administrative/security domain, separate network and credentials, independently protected monotonic state and append-only retention, authenticated mTLS identities, read versus append versus recovery roles, two-person recovery approval, signed checkpoint receipts with separate audit key custody, deployment/tenant/BU scope and sequence/digest/epoch/previous receipt identity. Provider must support atomic compare-and-append and durable acknowledgement; qualify against coordinated restore, stale/duplicate/forked receipts, key rotation, authority outage and independent restore. WORM object storage alone does not provide a linearizable CAS. Prefer separately administered authority service backed by qualified immutable retention and HSM-managed signing, customer selects provider/offline hosting; no particular vendor assumed. Define exact retention and legal holds with customer; no customer data or secrets in checkpoint. Full history verification at restore; incremental verification from authenticated checkpoint on normal path.

Cutover: acquire exclusive recovery fence; stop ingress/workers and drain or cancel in-flight operations, revoke DB network/credentials as necessary, advance authority to quarantine and retain current audit/licence heads; restore isolated primary, local licence/recovery state and identity config, validate full checkpoint and epoch; if mismatch, preserve evidence and require approved recovery, never lower authority floor; rotate/rebind DB epoch and credentials, verify all paths, two-person signed activation, then reopen ingress. In-flight operations admitted before fencing may have delivered data; no claim of retroactive erasure. Use bounded deadlines and explicit fencing leases; production enforcement and verification pending. If primary commit without anchor, deny new protected operations, reconcile from independently authenticated commit/outbox evidence under quarantine; no arbitrary tail adoption. Signed receipt service, external custody, event schema/version and privacy, atomic real CRM mutation/outbox, durable dispatch/idempotency, full restore, crash/failover and all-path enforcement remain open.
